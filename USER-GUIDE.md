# DearDish User Guide

DearDish helps you enjoy restaurant-style meals even when you are managing a health condition, an allergy, or a special diet. You pick a dish you like, and DearDish rewrites it as a cookable recipe built around your health profile.

This guide walks you through everything a user can do. No technical knowledge is needed.

## What you need

- A web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge) on a computer or phone.
- An email address you can access.
- A few minutes to set up your health profile.

## The basics

DearDish has five screens you will use:

- **Menu**: the list of dishes you can adapt.
- **Dish page**: the details of one dish, with a button to start the adaptation.
- **Adapt page**: where your personalized recipe appears.
- **My Meals**: recipes you have saved.
- **Profile**: your health details.

The top of every page has the DearDish name and links to move between these screens.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open DearDish in your browser. You land on the welcome page.
2. Click **Register**.
3. Type your email address.
4. Type a password of at least 8 characters.
5. Retype the same password in the **Confirm password** field.
6. Click **Register**.

If something is wrong, a message appears next to the field explaining what to fix (for example, the password is too short, or the passwords do not match). Fix it and try again. DearDish never saves an account that has an error.

Already have an account? Click **Sign in** instead.

## 2. Sign in and sign out

**To sign in:**

1. Click **Sign in** in the top menu (or on the welcome page).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

You land on the Menu. If your email or password is wrong, a message tells you so, and no one gets signed in.

**To sign out:**

1. Click **Log out** in the top menu.

Signing out keeps your profile and saved meals private. When you come back, you sign in the same way.

## 3. Set up your health profile

Your health profile is what makes DearDish personalized. The more you tell it, the better your recipes fit you. You can change it any time.

1. Click **Profile** in the top menu.
2. Fill in the fields you want:
   - **Conditions**: health conditions to work around, for example "type 2 diabetes" or "high blood pressure".
   - **Allergies**: foods to avoid completely, for example "peanuts, shellfish".
   - **Dietary preferences**: how you like to eat, for example "vegetarian" or "low carb".
   - **Notes**: anything else, for example "cooks for two, no oven".
3. Click **Save profile**.

The first three fields hold a short line each (up to 200 characters). Notes can be longer (up to 2000 characters). Your profile stays saved and reappears when you come back.

If you save without filling anything in, that is fine. DearDish will then use general healthy-cooking guidance for you.

## 4. Browse the menu

1. Click **Menu** in the top menu.
2. You see a grid of restaurant-style dishes. Each card shows the cuisine, the dish name, and a short description.
3. Click a dish to open its page.

## 5. Adapt a dish to your needs

1. On a dish page, read the ingredients list.
2. Click **Adapt this dish**.
3. A message appears saying DearDish is adapting your dish. The recipe appears word by word as it is written. Wait for it to finish.

Your adapted recipe includes a new title, an ingredient list that notes every substitution made for you, and numbered cooking steps. It honors your allergies, respects your dietary preferences, steers around ingredients that clash with your conditions, and folds in your notes.

If the recipe cannot be prepared, a plain message appears and you can click **Adapt this dish** again to retry.

You can adapt the same dish as many times as you like. Each adaptation is generated fresh for your current profile.

## 6. Save a meal

After a recipe finishes:

1. Find the **Save as meal** box below the recipe.
2. The **Title** field is filled in for you. Change it if you like (up to 200 characters).
3. Click **Save as meal**.

DearDish saves the recipe to **My Meals** and takes you there. The save button stays greyed out until a recipe has finished, so there is nothing to save before that.

## 7. Review and delete saved meals

1. Click **My Meals** in the top menu.
2. Each saved meal shows its title, the date you saved it, and a preview of the recipe.
3. Click **Delete** on a meal to remove it.

Only you can see your meals. If you delete one by mistake it is gone, so delete with care.

## Tips

- Fill in your profile before adapting. It makes every recipe more useful.
- Be specific in **Allergies**. List each ingredient you must avoid, separated by commas.
- Use **Notes** for practical things: how many people you cook for, equipment you have, or foods you dislike.
- Adapt a dish again after you update your profile to get a version that matches the change.

## Troubleshooting

**I do not know my password.** DearDish has no password reset in this demo. Create a new account with a different email.

**The recipe did not appear.** Check your internet connection, then click **Adapt this dish** again.

**My meal did not save.** Give it a title with at least one character, then click **Save as meal** again.

**I cannot reach my Menu, Meals, or Profile.** These screens need you to be signed in. Click **Sign in** and enter your details.

**The recipe mentions a food I avoid.** Add that food to your **Allergies** field, save, and adapt the dish again.

## A note on privacy

Your email, health profile, and saved meals are tied to your account and shown only to you when you are signed in. This is a demo app, so treat it as a demonstration and avoid entering sensitive medical details you would not want stored.
